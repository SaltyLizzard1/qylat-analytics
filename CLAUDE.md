# CLAUDE.md

## Color rules

Do not use cream, gold, tan, or any warm tint anywhere in this project, now or in future changes.

Forbidden values (not exhaustive): #FBF6E3, #E8C84A, #8B6914, #2D1A00, #1A1008, #231409,
#3A2210, #0F0A05, #8A7A60, #E6DCC3, #F3ECD6, #F9F4E8, #FFF8E1, #FFE082, #D4C4A0,
#C9A030, #F5E070, #8A7A60, #4A3820, #5A4A30, #6A5A40, #3A281A, #92A882.

The design is plain black and white. Permitted palette:
- Page and card background: #FFFFFF
- Card border: 1px solid #D0D0D0
- Primary text: #111111
- Secondary text: #555555
- Neutral boxes / table stripes: #F2F2F2
- Primary buttons: background #111111, text #FFFFFF, border 1px solid #111111
- Severity styles only via lib/severity.ts

## Typography

No Cormorant Garamond. No Google Fonts. Use system-ui everywhere.
Headings: font-weight 600, color #111111.

## No em dashes

Do not use em dashes in code, comments, or strings.
